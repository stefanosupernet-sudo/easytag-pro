Transfers to the United States and the Data Privacy Framework

The United States is not an adequate country as a whole. A transfer is covered only when it goes to a commercial organisation on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework list, under the European Commission’s adequacy decision of 10 July 2023. The official list is kept by the US Department of Commerce: dataprivacyframework.gov/list. While the recipient’s status is Active, Article 45 GDPR does not require a second transfer tool.

The decision is in force. The General Court of the European Union dismissed the main challenge on 3 September 2025 (case T-553/23). An appeal is pending before the Court of Justice (C-703/25 P). An appeal does not suspend the decision. If the decision is annulled or suspended, this section will no longer be enough and we will update it.

4. Cookies

Before you choose, we do not load Google Analytics, Google Ads or any other measurement tag. Accept stores the permission in the et_consent cookie. Reject stores the refusal: the site stays usable and measurement stays off. Refusing is as easy as accepting. The Cookie button at the bottom left opens the choice again. You can also delete et_consent in the browser.

The choice cookie is necessary. Any visit statistics would rely on consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and the ePrivacy rules). That tag is not installed today: pressing Accept does not, by itself, set a Google cookie.

You may lodge a complaint with the authority in the country where you live. In Italy that is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it). The lead authority for the controller, which is established in Poland, is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl.
