The starting point: Krakow, since 2019

EasyTag Pro comes from the experience of an Italian team that, starting in 2019, worked at the Google offices in Krakow on tags and digital measurement. This is not a generic marketing specialization, but day-to-day work on the implementations that get the right data into Google tools: conversions, analytics, feeds and consent signals.

In those years the Italian market kept asking for support on concrete cases: sites with duplicate tags, e-commerce stores with untracked purchases, Ads campaigns that were not receiving reliable conversions, Analytics properties that were hard to read, incomplete Merchant Center feeds, cookie banners not correctly connected to the tags. The team handled those requests by building a method based on verification, documentation and repeatable solutions.

Today EasyTag Pro is the spin-off of that path: the same technical expertise, made directly available to business owners, technical offices and web agencies that do not want to (or cannot) build an in-house team dedicated only to tagging.

What we handled for the Italian market

The scope of the work has always been broad, because in the field measurement problems rarely stay isolated. We have worked on:

  • Google Ads native tags — conversions, Conversion Linker, dynamic values and alignment between conversion actions and business goals. Deep dive: Google Ads native tag.
  • Google Analytics 4 — events, parameters, e-commerce and flow validation. Deep dive: Google Analytics 4.
  • Consent Mode and operational privacy — the connection between user choices, the CMP and Google tag behavior. Deep dive: Consent Mode. Product page: Consent Mode v2.
  • Merchant Center — consistency between product data, tracking and the signals used by Shopping and Performance Max campaigns. Product page: Dynamic Remarketing.
  • DataLayer and Google Tag Manager — a stable data infrastructure, not tied to page layout alone. Deep dive: DataLayer architecture.

This overall view matters: an Ads tag that is “correct” on a page with poorly managed consent, or a GA4 event without a consistent DataLayer, produces fragile reports. The value of the Krakow team was precisely keeping these pieces together.

Cookies, scripts and CMP: the context we work in

Measuring today also means respecting the privacy framework and the way the browser runs code. Cookies are not a technical detail to leave only to the banner: they influence what tags can read, for how long and on what basis. Anyone who runs a site needs to know what they install and why.

In the same way, much of the tracking goes through JavaScript: GTM snippets, DataLayer pushes, form and checkout callbacks. If the script is loaded poorly, late or in conflict with other modules, the data arrives incomplete even when “the tag seems to be on the page”.

That is where the CMP (Consent Management Platform) and Google Consent Mode come in: consent signals must talk to the tags, not live in a separate silo. A serious implementation connects the user’s choices to tag state, avoids inconsistent firing and keeps tracking when consent is granted.

EasyTag Pro steps in exactly at that junction: not only “adding a snippet”, but making cookies, scripts and the CMP work together with the measurement the business needs.

A spin-off built for business owners and agencies

Many Italian companies need reliable tracking, but not a permanent headcount on GTM. Web agencies, for their part, often prefer to focus on creativity, media and the client relationship, and to delegate the more technical tagging work to a specialist.

EasyTag Pro fills that space: ready-made containers where it makes sense to standardize, custom projects where the site or the funnel requires them, and language that is clear both to people who buy media and to people who develop. We stay focused on Google Tag Manager, GA4, Ads tags, Consent Mode and related topics such as Merchant Center: we are not a generic full-service agency.

The result we aim for is operational: verifiable conversions, readable events, less dependence on fragile selectors, and documentation that is still useful after go-live.

FAQ about EasyTag Pro

Are you really connected to the Google Krakow experience?

Yes. EasyTag Pro is the spin-off of the Italian team active since 2019 at the Google offices in Krakow on tags and measurement, with a long-standing focus on the needs of the Italian market.

Which Google products do you work on?

In particular Google Ads native tags, Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, Consent Mode, topics linked to Merchant Center, and DataLayer architecture. We do not replace creative campaign management: we make the measurement that feeds it reliable.

Do you work only with large companies?

No. We work with SMEs, e-commerce businesses and web agencies that want specialist expertise without building an in-house team right away. Ready-made containers cover standard cases; custom projects cover sites with particular logic.

Do you also handle cookie banners and CMPs?

We handle the connection between the CMP, consent and Google tags (Consent Mode and consistent firing). Legal privacy advice stays with the client and their advisors; we make what the tags do technically consistent with the user’s choices.

How can I get started?

You can start from the shop for a ready-made container, or write to us from the Contact page for an audit or a custom collaboration.
